# Novaya Gazeta and Dmitry Muratov hand over the Nobel Medal in favor of Ukrainian refugees

![](_assets/a1/a1a520d3d7144482bfd8e3be7730bb15.webp)

*Фото: AFP/East News*

**What needs to be done right away:  **

- Stop combat fire.
- Exchange prisoners.
- Release the bodies of the dead.
- Provide humanitarian corridors and assistance.
- Support refugees.

**What can we do:** To share with refugees, the wounded and children who need urgent treatment what is dear to you and has a value for others.

Novaya Gazeta and I have decided to donate the 2021 Nobel Peace Prize Medal to the Ukrainian Refugee Fund. There are already over 10 million of refugees. I ask the auction houses to respond and put up for auction this world-famous award.

**Dmitry Muratov**, editor-in-chief of Novaya Gazeta, 2021 Nobel Peace Prize winner.
